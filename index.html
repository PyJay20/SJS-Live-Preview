About Us

St Joseph’s R.C. School is a public, co-educational English-medium school located on private property. Rooted in strong Christian values, it is a “family school” that promotes love, care, & a supportive environment. The principal & staff are committed to fostering a joyful & cooperative atmosphere where learning thrives with minimal obstacles. Our goal is to make St Joseph’s a place where children truly want to be.

About our
Teachers

The teachers at St Joseph’s R.C. School are dedicated to building strong, positive relationships with each child. They work to create a balanced programme that combines both academics & sports, aiming to nurture responsibility, leadership, sportsmanship, thoughtfulness, & mutual respect among learners. Open & ongoing communication between parents, staff & pupils, helps ensure a supportive & collaborative learning environment for every child.
