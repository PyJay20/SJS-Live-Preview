❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What time does school start?

School starts promptly at 7:50 AM. Gates open at 7:00 AM.

How do I notify the school of an absence?

You may call the office before 8:30 AM or as soon as possible in case of an emergency.

Where can I buy uniforms?

Uniforms are available from our helpful staff member Charmelle Human at the school office.
